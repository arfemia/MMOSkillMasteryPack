The Enchanting mastery track switches on, priced in Void essence. Fishing and Acrobatics stay parked. Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer, the same as 2.0.1.

- **New: the Enchanting mastery track is live.** Four steps of Enchanting loot luck: 3% at the first, 5% at the second, 10% at the third, and a repeating step at half a percent a purchase. It opens at Enchanting level 5.
- **Priced in Void essence.** The first three steps ask for 1, 2 and 3 Void essence, the third also takes a single Voidheart, and the repeating step asks for 2 Void essence a purchase. The mastery points and Life Essence on every step are unchanged.
- **Fishing and Acrobatics stay switched off**, with the prices they had. A server owner can still turn either on from `mastery.json`.
- **Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer.** No floor moves.
