The Enchanting mastery track switches on, priced in Void essence. Fishing and Acrobatics stay parked. Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer, the same as 2.0.1.

- **New: the Enchanting mastery track is live.** Four steps of Enchanting loot luck: 3% at the first, 5% at the second, 10% at the third, and a repeating step at half a percent a purchase. It opens at Enchanting level 5.
- **Priced in Essence of the Void.** The first three steps ask for 1, 2 and 3 Essence of the Void, the third also takes a single Voidheart, and the repeating step asks for 2 Essence of the Void a purchase. The mastery points on every step are unchanged.
- **Every mastery step asks for half again as much Essence of Life.** A step that wanted 600 wants 900, and the three essence quests follow: Essence Collector asks for 150, Essence Devotee for 750 and the Trainer's Due for 115. Mastery points, the other essences and anything you have already bought are unchanged; the new price applies to the steps you have not bought yet.
- **Fishing and Acrobatics stay switched off.** A server owner can still turn either on from `mastery.json`.
- **The Mastery Trainer's words, rewritten.** Their greeting and quest text read plainer, and two quests take new names: First Step Toward Mastery becomes The First Milestone, and Path of Mastery becomes Many Trades. What each quest asks and pays is unchanged, and so is your progress.
- **The mastery achievements say what they ask for.** Five of them name the level or amount they want (level 15 or 60 in any skill, 300 Mastery Points earned, 100 or 10,000 Essence of Life picked up), and Devotee of Mastery says it wants every other mastery achievement. Apprentice of the Path and Journeyman of the Path become Mastery Apprentice and Mastery Journeyman. Your progress and rewards are unchanged.
- **Defense Mastery is renamed Block Mastery**, the skill's name in game. Your purchases are unchanged.
- **The Trainer's Due says everything it asks for.** Its text mentioned only essence; it now names the Linen Scraps and the kills as well. The trainer's first words are shorter, and the repeating steps on the gathering and utility tracks no longer end with "Grind without limit."
- **Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer.** No floor moves.
