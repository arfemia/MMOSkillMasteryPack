This one moves the Skill Mastery Pack onto Hytale Update 7, and your Mastery Points wait in the achievement book for you to collect. It needs an Update 7 server, MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### Mastery Points

- **Your Mastery Points wait in the achievement book.** Every skill still pays one at level 15, 30, 45 and on up to 195. Each one now waits in the Mastery section of your achievement book until you collect it, with each skill's levels listed together, and the skill's rewards page no longer lists them. Each one also counts 10 achievement points, and a skill a pack adds gets the same levels.
- **Already past some of those levels? Their points are waiting.** The next time you log in, every level you have already reached offers its point in the book, including any you collected from a skill's rewards page before.

### For server owners

- **Switching mastery off hides the whole ladder.** With the mastery feature off, none of these levels shows in the book.
- **Retuned Mastery Points in your own `command-rewards.json`?** Take those entries out. The pack no longer pays Mastery Points through skill rewards, so the achievement book is where a level's point comes from. To change what a level pays, ship a file with the same name as the pack's `Mastery_Point_` file for that level in your own pack.
