### Mastery Points

- **Your Mastery Points wait in the achievement book.** Every skill still pays one at level 15, 30, 45 and on up to 195. Each one now waits in the Mastery section of your achievement book until you collect it, with each skill's levels listed together, and the skill's rewards page no longer lists them.
- **Already past some of those levels? Their points are waiting.** The next time you log in, every level you have already reached offers its point in the book, including any you collected from a skill's rewards page before.
