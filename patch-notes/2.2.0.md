This one gives the Mastery Trainer a board of daily lessons and a standing you raise with the trainer, and every rank you reach pays Mastery Points. It needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, the same as 2.1.1.

### The Mastery Trainer's lessons

- **Added the Mastery Trainer's lessons.** Ask the trainer in the Forgotten Temple "Do you have lessons for me?" to open the board. New lessons go up every day, all of them out in the world: fell logs, mine ore, catch fish, put down creatures, use your abilities, craft at a bench. Collect each one at the board for some Essence of Life and standing with the trainer.
- **Added a standing with the Mastery Trainer.** It climbs from Newcomer through Student, Trusted Student, Adept and Master, then on to Master II, Master III and Grandmaster. Ask the trainer "How am I doing?" to see your rank and what the next one opens.
- Every rank from Student up pays Mastery Points: 3, 5, 10, 15, 15, 20 and 25 on the way to Grandmaster. They wait in the Mastery section of your achievement book until you collect them, and Grandmaster also gives you the Grandmaster title.
- Higher ranks open more of the board. Student adds a Crafting lesson and Adept a Hard one. Master adds a Master's lesson, which pays a Mastery Point each time you finish it, and Grandmaster adds a second.
- Added A Week of Lessons. Once you have met the trainer it shows up in your log every week: finish any five lessons and collect extra standing from the trainer.
- The trainer's other quests now raise your standing too, Essence Devotee most of all.
- Lessons count as bounties, so they move your bounty statistics and your daily bounty streak.

### The Mastery Tithe

- The Mastery Tithe is retired, and the lessons take its place. If you are partway through one, you can still finish it and collect its point.
- Paid the Tithe before? What You Already Paid shows up in your log. Talk to the trainer and you start at Trusted Student, with the Student and Trusted Student points waiting in your book.

### For server owners

- To change what a rank pays, ship a file with the same name as that rank's achievement in your own pack. To add a lesson, ship a board contract that names the trainer's board.
