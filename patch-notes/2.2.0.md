The Mastery Trainer has daily lessons for you, and Mastery Trainer's Favor, your standing with the trainer, pays Mastery Points at every rank up to Grandmaster.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### New

- Added the Mastery Trainer's lessons. Ask the trainer in the Forgotten Temple "Do you have lessons for me?" to open the board. New lessons go up every day, all of them out in the world: fell logs, mine ore, catch fish, put down creatures, use your abilities, craft at a bench. Collect each one at the board for 10 to 40 Essence of Life and standing with the trainer.
- Added Mastery Trainer's Favor, your standing with the trainer. It climbs from Newcomer through Student, Trusted Student, Adept and Master, then on to Master II, Master III and Grandmaster. Ask the trainer "How am I doing?" to see your rank and what the next one opens.
- Every rank from Student up pays Mastery Points: 3, 5, 10, 15, 15, 20 and 25 on the way to Grandmaster, which also gives you the Grandmaster title. Collect them in the Mastery section of your achievement book.
- Higher ranks open more of the board. Student adds a Crafting lesson and Adept a Hard one. Master adds a Master's lesson, worth a Mastery Point each time you finish it, and Grandmaster a second.
- Added A Week of Lessons. Once you have met the trainer, or collected What You Already Paid, it shows up in your log every week: finish any five lessons and collect extra standing from the trainer.
- Lessons count as bounties, so they move your bounty statistics and your daily bounty streak.

### Changes

- The trainer's other quests now raise your standing too, Essence Devotee most of all.
- Mastery Points from skill levels now wait in the Mastery section of your achievement book until you collect them. Every skill still pays one at level 15, 30, 45 and on up to 195, with each skill's levels listed together, and the skill's rewards page no longer lists them. Each one counts 10 achievement points, and a skill a pack adds gets the same levels.
- Already past some of those levels? The next time you log in, every level you have reached offers its point in the book, including any you collected from a skill's rewards page before.
- The Mastery Tithe is retired, and the lessons take its place. If you are partway through one, you can still finish it and collect its point.
- Paid the Tithe before? What You Already Paid shows up in your log. Talk to the trainer and you start at Trusted Student, with the Student and Trusted Student points waiting in your book.

### For server owners

- Switching the mastery feature off hides the whole ladder: none of these levels shows in the book.
- Retuned Mastery Points in your own `command-rewards.json`? Take those entries out, since the pack no longer pays them through skill rewards. To change what a level pays, ship a file with the same name as the pack's `Mastery_Point_` file for that level in your own pack.
- To change what a rank pays, ship a file with the same name as that rank's achievement in your own pack. To add a lesson, ship a board contract that names the trainer's board.
