- **The Enchanting mastery track switches on.** `enchanting_mastery` (nodes `enc_t1`, `enc_t2`, `enc_t3` and the repeating `enc_eternal`) has shipped parked since 2.0.0 and is live from this version for every server that has the pack. Its four steps still add Enchanting loot luck, at the same sizes as before, and the mastery point part of every price is unchanged (the Life Essence part rises with every other track's, below). The item part of the price is now Void essence, the essence the Void school already sells its steps for: 1, 2 and 3 on the three steps and 2 a purchase on the repeating node, with a single Voidheart added at the third step in place of the two Purple Crystals the parked version asked for. Acrobatics and Fishing stay parked; their mastery point and item prices are unchanged, and their Life Essence rises with every other track's (below).
- **The utility generator switches tracks on one at a time.** `Utility_Skill_Masteries` takes each track's on/off state (`enabled`) and the size of its third-step extra (`t3ExtraCount`) from its own row, so turning the next utility track on is a one-word change on that row and its price can differ from the other two. `Enabled` lands as a real boolean and the count as a number; `MasteryGeneratorTypedTokenTest` in the plugin pins both. No new file: the track is still written by the same generator, so a server owner's `mastery.json` override or a same-named `Masteries/` file works on it exactly as before.
- **Purchases carry over.** No node id changed, so anyone who bought an Enchanting node through an owner override keeps it; the price of the steps they have not bought yet is the new one.
- **Every `life_essence` price rises by half.** All 129 `Cost.Currencies.life_essence` rows under `Server/MMOSkillTree/Masteries/` and in the three generators (`Combat_Skill_Masteries`, `Gathering_Skill_Masteries`, `Utility_Skill_Masteries`) are multiplied by 1.5 and rounded to a clean step: 200 to 300, 250 to 375, 300 to 450, 350 to 525, 400 to 600, 500 to 750, 600 to 900, 1,200 to 1,800, 1,500 to 2,250, 2,200 to 3,300, 3,500 to 5,250, 4,000 to 6,000, 4,800 to 7,200, 6,000 to 9,000, 8,000 to 12,000. `mastery_point` and the item parts are unchanged. The three essence quests follow: `Pack_Essence_Collector` `Amount` 100 to 150, `Pack_Essence_Devotee` 500 to 750, `Mastery_Tribute`'s essence step 75 to 115. `Pack_Essence_Hoarder_100` and `_10000` are milestones, not asks, and are unchanged. A bought node stays bought at whatever it cost.
- **The mastery achievements carry their amounts.** `Pack_First_Mastery_Milestone`, `Pack_Mastery_Milestone_Journeyman`, `Pack_Mastery_Hoarder_300`, `Pack_Essence_Hoarder_100` and `Pack_Essence_Hoarder_10000` gain `Text.TextArgs.Flavor: ["@amount"]`, and their `.desc` keys carry `{0}`, filled with the first criterion's `Amount` (zc's `AchievementDefinition` and the plugin's `CommonAchievementFold` both read it there), so a retune of an `Amount` changes the line in every language with no new translation. The two milestone titles read Mastery Apprentice and Mastery Journeyman, and `achievement.pack_mastery_devotee.desc` asks for every other mastery achievement: Essence Hoarder I is not one of its `MetaChildren`, but the 10,000 pickups Essence Hoarder II counts include its 100. Ids, criterion keys, points and rewards are unchanged, so progress carries over.
- **`mastery.defense_mastery.title` reads Block Mastery**, the plugin's name for `skill.defense`. The track id `defense_mastery` and its node ids are unchanged, so purchases carry over, and the composed node titles follow ("Block Mastery: Sharpened").
- **Trainer and track text.** `quest.mastery_tribute.flavor` names all three parts of the Trainer's Due (essence, Linen Scraps and the kills); `dialogue.mmo_mastery_trainer.first_words.text` is shorter and drops the `<color>` accent on "mastery", which the dialogue convention keeps for places and names to reach. The six gathering and utility `*_eternal.desc` keys (`acr`, `enc`, `fis`, `har`, `min`, `woo`) drop their closing "Grind without limit."
- The manifest floors stay at MMO Skill Tree `^1.6.1` and ZiggfreedCommon `>=2.1.0`: the typed-token substitution the switch relies on is already in the 2.1.0 library, and so is the `@amount` fill the achievement descriptions use.
