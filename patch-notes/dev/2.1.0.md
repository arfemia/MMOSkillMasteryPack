- **The Enchanting mastery track switches on.** `enchanting_mastery` (nodes `enc_t1`, `enc_t2`, `enc_t3` and the repeating `enc_eternal`) has shipped parked since 2.0.0 and is live from this version for every server that has the pack. Its four steps still add Enchanting loot luck, at the same sizes as before, and the mastery point and Life Essence part of every price is unchanged. The item part of the price is now Void essence, the essence the Void school already sells its steps for: 1, 2 and 3 on the three steps and 2 a purchase on the repeating node, with a single Voidheart added at the third step in place of the two Purple Crystals the parked version asked for. Acrobatics and Fishing stay parked, with the same prices they had.
- **The utility generator switches tracks on one at a time.** `Utility_Skill_Masteries` takes each track's on/off state (`enabled`) and the size of its third-step extra (`t3ExtraCount`) from its own row, so turning the next utility track on is a one-word change on that row and its price can differ from the other two. `Enabled` lands as a real boolean and the count as a number; `MasteryGeneratorTypedTokenTest` in the plugin pins both. No new file: the track is still written by the same generator, so a server owner's `mastery.json` override or a same-named `Masteries/` file works on it exactly as before.
- **Purchases carry over.** No node id changed, so anyone who bought an Enchanting node through an owner override keeps it; the price of the steps they have not bought yet is the new one.
- The manifest floors stay at MMO Skill Tree `^1.6.1` and ZiggfreedCommon `>=2.1.0`: the typed-token substitution the switch relies on is already in the 2.1.0 library.
