The Mastery Tithe gives way to the Mastery Trainer's reputation and its board of lessons. All of it is pack content on CommonLib's reputation, board and achievement engines; there is no code. `manifest.json` goes to `2.2.0`; `ServerVersion` and the dependencies are unchanged (`Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree ^1.7.1`, `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0`). The engine reads reputation groups once at start, so the new group arrives with the restart that installs the pack.

- **The reputation `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer`.** The native group `Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/Mmo_Mastery_Trainer.json` (`NPCGroups: []`, `InitialReputationValue: 0`) and its CommonLib companion `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/MMOSkillTree/Mmo_Mastery_Trainer.json`: `Text`, `Icon` `Ingredient_Crystal_Purple`, `Order` 1, `Ranks` naming the five shared ranks from Neutral to Exalted plus three of its own, `Mastery_Trainer_Tier_1` to `_3` at `From` 30000, 40000 and 50000, and four `Earn` lines. No `Kills`, `Gear`, `Cap` or `Beyond`. The reputation id and the rank ids are saved progress.
- **The board `Mmo_Mastery_Lessons`** (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Boards/MMOSkillTree/`): `Rotation` Daily, `Where` `ForgottenTemple`, `Order` 50, `Currencies` `Mastery_Point` and `Life_Essence`, and `Requires` the `mmoskilltree:feature` `mastery` factor (the hide axis) plus an `AnyOf` of `ziggfreedcommon:quest_completed` on `meet_the_mastery_trainer` or `mastery_tithe_credit` (the lock, so a past Tithe payer can open it at once). Six slots: `Mastery_Gathering` and `Mastery_Fighting` open, then `Mastery_Crafting` at `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer/Friendly`, `Mastery_Hard` at `/Revered`, and `Mastery_Master` at `/Exalted` and again at `/Mastery_Trainer_Tier_3`, each `Optional` with a `ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank` `Requires`. `Grades` names and colours the five bands.
- **Thirteen lessons** in `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Bounties/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/` on the Abstract `Mmo_Lesson_Base` (`Listing.Category` `bounty`, the mastery feature in `Requires`, `Rewards.Claim` the lootable `Mmo_Mastery_Lesson_Pay` and 250 `Reputation`). Gathering: `mmo_lesson_timber` (`BREAK_BLOCK` `Wood` CONTAINS, 40), `mmo_lesson_ore` (`BREAK_BLOCK` `Ore_` PREFIX, 40), `mmo_lesson_fishing` (`CATCH_FISH`, 8). Fighting: `mmo_lesson_hunt` (`KILL_ENTITY`, 25), `mmo_lesson_abilities` (`ABILITY_CAST`, 25). Crafting: `mmo_lesson_crafting` (`CRAFT_ITEM`, 15), `mmo_lesson_workshop` (`GAIN_XP` `CRAFTING`, 2000). Hard, 500 standing: `mmo_lesson_hard_timber` (120 logs), `mmo_lesson_hard_hunt` (75), `mmo_lesson_hard_study` (`GAIN_XP` any skill, 12000). Master's, 500 standing plus one `mastery_point`: `mmo_lesson_master_ore` (240), `mmo_lesson_master_hunt` (150), `mmo_lesson_master_study` (24000). The log and ore steps carry their own `TextKey`.
- **`Lootables/Mmo_Mastery_Lesson_Pay.json`**: one `Expected` roll of 10 to 40 `Ingredient_Life_Essence`.
- **Seven rank achievements** in `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Achievements/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/Trainer/`: `mastery_trainer_friendly`, `_honored`, `_revered`, `_exalted`, `_tier_1`, `_tier_2` and `_tier_3`. Each has one `REPUTATION_RANK` criterion `reach-rank` (`Target` the rank id, `Qualifier` `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer`, its own `TextKey`), `Category` `mastery`, the chain `mastery_trainer_ranks` at tiers 1 to 7, `Points` 10, 10, 25, 25, 25, 50 and 50, and `Rewards.Claim` paying `mastery_point` 3, 5, 10, 15, 15, 20 and 25 through the `Currency` kind. `mastery_trainer_tier_3` also pays the title `Mmo_Mastery_Grandmaster` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Titles/MMOSkillTree/`) in `Rewards.Auto`.
- **Two quests** in `Quests/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/`. `mastery_tithe_credit` ("What You Already Paid"): `AutoAccept`, `Requires` the mastery feature plus `ziggfreedcommon:quest_completions` on `pack_mastery_tithe` `Min` 1, one `TALK_TO_NPC` step on `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer`, `TurnInAt` the trainer, `Rewards.Claim` 3000 `Reputation`. `mastery_lesson_week` ("A Week of Lessons"): `AutoAccept`, `Repeat.Reset` Weekly, `Requires` the same `AnyOf` as the board, one `COMPLETE_BOUNTY` step on `Mmo_Mastery_Lessons` (Amount 5, any band), `TurnInAt` the trainer, `Rewards.Claim` 1000 `Reputation`.
- **Reputation rows on six quests' `Rewards.Claim`**: `meet_the_mastery_trainer` 250, `pack_first_milestone` 250, `mastery_tribute` 250, `pack_essence_collector` 500, `pack_path_of_mastery` 1000, `pack_essence_devotee` 1500. Nothing else in them changes.
- **`pack_mastery_tithe` is retired in place.** `Flow.AutoAccept` false, `Listing.Hidden` true and `Repeat.MaxCompletions` 1; its id, `Requires`, `Objectives` and `Rewards` stay. A player carrying it can finish and collect it, nobody is handed it again, and a player who collected it before reads it as completed for good, so the Objective Book never offers it back. Its completion record is what `mastery_tithe_credit` reads.
- **The trainer's conversation** (`Dialogues/MMOSkillTree/Mmo_Mastery_Trainer.json`): the menu moves into a `trainer_menu` fragment shared by `menu`, `ready_generic` and the new `lessons_fresh`, and gains "Do you have lessons for me?" (opens the board, once `meet_the_mastery_trainer` or `mastery_tithe_credit` is collected, an `AnyOf` the `lessons_fresh` beat shares) and "How am I doing?" (opens the trainer's Reputation page, `ziggfreedcommon:reputation` `Min` 1). Two new `Start.First` beats: `tithe_credit` while `mastery_tithe_credit` is active (`MarkTalked`, then the quest list on that quest) and `lessons_fresh` once a day (`Once` `Period` Daily, `OnceId` `lessons_fresh`, paying nothing). `Start.Quests` gains rows for `mastery_tithe_credit` and `mastery_lesson_week`.
- **New keys** in `mmoskilltree.lang`: `quest.*` for the two quests and thirteen lessons, `objective.text.mastery_lesson.*` (three step lines), `board.mmo_mastery_lessons.*` (title, description, five grades), `reputation.mmo_mastery_trainer.*` (name, flavor, eight ranks, four earn lines), `achievement.mastery_trainer_*` and `objective.mastery_trainer.*` (seven each), `title.mmo_mastery_grandmaster.*`, and `dialogue.mmo_mastery_trainer.*` (two menu options, the tithe line and its option, the daily line). English only so far.
