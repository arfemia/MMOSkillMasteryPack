### Income and the trainer's quests

- **Mastery Points come only from one-time content.** The pack pays 29 points in all, each once: A Word with the Trainer 1, The First Milestone 2, Many Trades 10, Essence Collector 2, Essence Devotee 5, Mastery Apprentice 1, Mastery Journeyman 3 and Devotee of Mastery 5. Nothing that repeats pays a point. The carried `pack_mastery_tithe`, never handed out, still pays its one point once to a player who holds it.
- **The trainer's ranks pay Essence of Life and a title.** Each of the seven `mastery_trainer_*` achievements pays 200 Essence of Life for each step up the ladder (200 at Student, 1400 at Grandmaster) as a Claim, and a title through `Rewards.Auto`: six new titles under `Titles/MMOSkillTree/` (`Mmo_Mastery_Student`, `_Trusted_Student`, `_Adept`, `_Master`, `_Master_II`, `_Master_III`), and `Mmo_Mastery_Grandmaster` at the top, which now ships in MMO Skill Tree under the same id, so the pack's copy is gone. Ids, criteria and progress are unchanged.
- **The hoarding achievements pay a title.** `pack_essence_hoarder_100`, `pack_essence_hoarder_10000` and `pack_mastery_hoarder_300` pay `Mmo_Mastery_Essence_Gatherer`, `Mmo_Mastery_Essence_Hoarder` and `Mmo_Mastery_Studious` and no points.
- **Repeating content pays the trainer's materials.** `Lootables/Mmo_Mastery_Focus_Materials.json` draws a Voidheart or `Mmo_Mastery_Element_Pair.json` (two of one element's essence: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Water or Void, picked at random). The Trainer's Due (`Mmo_Mastery_Offering`, its 40% roll) and the three Master's lessons pay it where they paid a point; the lessons board's balance strip shows Essence of Life only.
- **The Essence Infusion moves to the Bounty Contracts pack** under its id, `Shop_Convert_Mastery_Point`, so purchase counts carry over: 500 Bounty Tokens for `Mmo_Mastery_Element_Pair`, three a day, gated on this pack being installed.
- **The First Oath** (`mastery_first_oath`): offered by the trainer once the meeting or What You Already Paid is collected; one talk step with the Qualifier `First_Oath`, credited only by the trainer's sworn line (a `MarkTalked` with the same Qualifier), which shows while `ziggfreedcommon:track_stance` on `Mmo_Focus` reads 1 or more. Pays 250 Essence of Life and 500 standing. The talk steps of `meet_the_mastery_trainer` and `mastery_tithe_credit` now write `Qualifier` `""` (an unqualified credit only), so the two kinds of credit never cross; their ids and progress are unchanged.
- **The Second Oath** (`mastery_second_oath`): taken with a finished Focus (`ziggfreedcommon:track_picks_taken` on `Mmo_Focus_*_Honed_II`), an oath sworn, and `Defense/Mmo_Focus_Slot_II` not yet owned (all asked when it is taken); 30 creatures defeated while an oath is sworn (the kill step's own `While` on `ziggfreedcommon:track_stance` `Mmo_Focus`, asked at every kill), then back to the trainer; pays only the `Pick` reward kind teaching `Mmo_Focus_Slot_II` on Defense, collected as a Claim.
- **The Mastery Trainer is a trainer.** Both placements carry a `Trainer` block (Learn, Quests and Services, no `Tracks`), so the `Trainer` destination and a pick's `Where` find them.
- **The trainer's temple standing comes back by itself when the Adventurers' Hall is gone.** `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer_Temple` ships switched on with `ziggfreedcommon:hub_district` `Mmo_Adventurers_Hall` `Max 0` in its `Requires`, and `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer_Hall` the same reading with `Min 1`, so the trainer stands and teaches in exactly one of the two; an owner no longer switches the temple standing on by hand.
- **The trainer's conversation** offers both oath quests on screens of their own, explains a Focus, an oath and the second slot (What is a Focus?), and its Teach me a Focus line opens the `Trainer` destination in place of the 1.x mastery page. A menu line, I want to swear or break an oath (`dialogue.mmo_mastery_trainer.menu.opt.oath`), opens the stance page on the oath set (`{ "Type": "Track_Stance", "Stance": "Mmo_Focus" }`).

### Focus signatures

- **Every Focus has a signature**, opened by its Signature step (`<Home track>/Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Signature`) and live only while that Focus is sworn. Fourteen are CommonLib procs under `Procs/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/`: one abstract skeleton, `Mmo_Focus_Signature` (`Trigger` Hit, `Hit.OverTime` false, `Order` 10 so a signature runs after every talent's proc on the same hit), and one child a Focus, `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Signature` with `Parent` `Mmo_Focus_Signature`, holding only its `Requires` (`ziggfreedcommon:track_pick` on the Signature step, which reads only a sworn Focus since stances), its `Like` (the Focus's own damage line), a weapon's `Holder` (`MmoWeaponSkill`), its wait and its actions. A pack adds a signature for its own Focus with one more child file.
- **The procs:** Artillery's Demolition Expert (an Artillery hit where the Projectile line counts flinches the struck foe and every foe within 3 blocks of it, every 6 s), Blunt's Skullbreaker (a charged mace swing staggers for 1 s, `Zig_Staggered`, 12 s a foe), Polearms' Spearpoint (a hit at 2.5 blocks or more roots for 1 s, `Root`, 12 s a foe), Staves' Quarterstaff (a staff swing pushes every foe within 2.5 blocks back about 2 blocks, every 10 s), Swords' Masterwork Blade (a sword `Crit` breaks the foe's guard and makes it flinch, 6 s a foe), Unarmed's Iron Body (a fist combo's `Finisher` gives back 2 stamina, every 6 s), Ice's Deep Freeze (15% on an Ice hit to freeze for 1 s, `Ability_Frost_Freeze`, 10 s a foe), Lightning's Thunderhead (staggers the struck foe and every foe within 2.5 blocks for 0.5 s, every 10 s), Water's High Tide (pushes back about 2 blocks and slows for 1.5 s, `Ability_Frost_Slow`, 10 s a foe), Arcane's Leyline (slows to half speed for 2 s, `Slow`, 10 s a foe), Void's Event Horizon (pulls the struck foe and every foe within 4 blocks about a block toward you, every 10 s), Poison's Blightbloom (a killing Poison hit spreads `Poison_T1` at half damage to up to 3 foes within 3 blocks, every 8 s), Earth's Tectonic (lifts the foe about a block and a half, 10 s a foe) and Life's Transfusion (withers the foe for 2 s and heals the nearest hurt ally within 12 blocks for 5, never you, every 10 s). Every control lands through CommonLib's control landing by its effect's kind, a talent's, so a boss or an elite shrugs it; the poison and the heal are no control.
- **Five are ability mods** under `MMOSkillTree/AbilityMods/Mastery/`, one file each on the same shape (`TargetAbility`, `Talent.Pick` on the Signature step, `PerRank` false): Archery's Hawkshot (Piercing Shot +1 `pierceCount`, +5% `damageMultiplier`), Axes' Tempest (Tempest -2000 `cooldownMs`), Daggers' Voidwalk (Shadowstep -3000 `cooldownMs`), Fire's Pyroclasm (Fireball +1 `damageRadius`, -400 `cooldownMs`) and the Bulwark's Mountainshield (Shield Slam +500 stun `durationMs`, +200 `iframesDurationMs`). A sixth, `Mmo_Focus_Bulwark_Path_Guard`, is the Bulwark's Path guard face: Guardian's Call +40% `durationMs`. An ability mod reads its pick through MMO Skill Tree's talent read, which counts an owned pick until that read moves onto CommonLib's live count.
- **Names and lines:** `mastery.focus.<focus>.signature` and `.desc` in the pack's `mmoskilltree.lang`, one pair a Focus, for the Signature step's text; the lines carry no numbers. Every number is retuned, or a signature switched off, in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/procs.json` (procs) or by a same-id ability mod file.
