### Income and the trainer's quests

- **Mastery Points come only from one-time content.** The pack pays 29 points in all, each once: A Word with the Trainer 1, The First Milestone 2, Many Trades 10, Essence Collector 2, Essence Devotee 5, Mastery Apprentice 1, Mastery Journeyman 3 and Devotee of Mastery 5. Nothing that repeats pays a point. The carried `pack_mastery_tithe`, never handed out, still pays its one point once to a player who holds it.
- **The trainer's ranks pay Essence of Life and a title.** Each of the seven `mastery_trainer_*` achievements pays 200 Essence of Life for each step up the ladder (200 at Student, 1400 at Grandmaster) as a Claim, and a title through `Rewards.Auto`: six new titles under `Titles/MMOSkillTree/` (`Mmo_Mastery_Student`, `_Trusted_Student`, `_Adept`, `_Master`, `_Master_II`, `_Master_III`), and `Mmo_Mastery_Grandmaster` at the top, which now ships in MMO Skill Tree under the same id, so the pack's copy is gone. Ids, criteria and progress are unchanged.
- **The hoarding achievements pay a title.** `pack_essence_hoarder_100`, `pack_essence_hoarder_10000` and `pack_mastery_hoarder_300` pay `Mmo_Mastery_Essence_Gatherer`, `Mmo_Mastery_Essence_Hoarder` and `Mmo_Mastery_Studious` and no points.
- **Repeating content pays the trainer's materials.** `Lootables/Mmo_Mastery_Focus_Materials.json` draws a Voidheart or `Mmo_Mastery_Element_Pair.json` (two of one element's essence: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Water or Void, picked at random). The Trainer's Due (`Mmo_Mastery_Offering`, its 40% roll) and the three Master's lessons pay it where they paid a point; the lessons board's balance strip shows Essence of Life only.
- **The Essence Infusion moves to the Bounty Contracts pack** under its id, `Shop_Convert_Mastery_Point`, so purchase counts carry over: 500 Bounty Tokens for `Mmo_Mastery_Element_Pair`, three a day, gated on this pack being installed.
- **The First Oath** (`mastery_first_oath`): offered by the trainer once the meeting or What You Already Paid is collected; one talk step with the Qualifier `First_Oath`, credited only by the trainer's sworn line (a `MarkTalked` with the same Qualifier), which shows while `ziggfreedcommon:track_stance` on `Mmo_Focus` reads 1 or more. Pays 250 Essence of Life and 500 standing. The talk steps of `meet_the_mastery_trainer` and `mastery_tithe_credit` now write `Qualifier` `""` (an unqualified credit only), so the two kinds of credit never cross; their ids and progress are unchanged.
- **The Second Oath** (`mastery_second_oath`): taken with a finished Focus (`ziggfreedcommon:track_picks_taken` on `Mmo_Focus_*_Honed_II`), an oath sworn, and `Defense/Mmo_Focus_Slot_II` not yet owned (all asked when it is taken); 30 creatures defeated while an oath is sworn (the kill step's own `While` on `ziggfreedcommon:track_stance` `Mmo_Focus`, asked at every kill), then back to the trainer; pays only the `Pick` reward kind teaching `Mmo_Focus_Slot_II` on Defense, collected as a Claim.
- **The Mastery Trainer is a trainer.** Both placements carry a `Trainer` block (Learn, Quests and Services, no `Tracks`), so the `Trainer` destination and a pick's `Where` find them.
- **The trainer's temple standing comes back by itself when the Adventurers' Hall is gone.** `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer_Temple` ships switched on with `ziggfreedcommon:hub_district` `Mmo_Adventurers_Hall` `Max 0` in its `Requires`, and `Mmo_Mastery_Trainer_Hall` the same reading with `Min 1`, so the trainer stands and teaches in exactly one of the two; an owner no longer switches the temple standing on by hand.
- **The trainer's conversation** offers both oath quests on screens of their own, explains a Focus, an oath and the second slot (What is a Focus?), and its Teach me a Focus line opens the `Trainer` destination in place of the 1.x mastery page. A menu line, I want to swear or break an oath (`dialogue.mmo_mastery_trainer.menu.opt.oath`), opens the stance page on the oath set (`{ "Type": "Track_Stance", "Stance": "Mmo_Focus" }`).

### The Focuses

- **Nineteen Focuses, bought once and sworn to count.** Nine weapons (Archery, Artillery, Axes, Blunt, Daggers, Polearms, Staves, Swords, Unarmed), nine elements (Fire, Ice, Water, Lightning, Arcane, Void, Poison, Earth, Life) and the Bulwark. Each has five steps on its home skill's own track, bought from the Mastery Trainer with Mastery Points and Essence of Life: Vow (level 30), Path (level 50, an either-or of `Path_Line` and `Path_Guard` sharing the Node `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Path`), Signature (level 70), Honed I (level 90) and Honed II (level 100). Step ids are `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_<Vow|Path_Line|Path_Guard|Signature|Honed_I|Honed_II>`. Homes: each weapon on its own skill, Fire on Fire, Ice and Water on Ice, Lightning on Lightning, Arcane on Arcane, Earth and Poison on Earth, Void on Void, Life on Life, the Bulwark on Defense.
- **One generator, one row a Focus.** `TrackPickGenerators/MMOSkillTree/Mmo_Focus_Steps.json` walks the set `Focus` (`GeneratorRows/MMOSkillTree/Focus/`, one row a Focus: `Focus`, `Key`, `Home`, `Icon`, and a weapon's `Line` where it is Projectile) against six inline step rows. Each row names a kind row as its `Parent` (`Mmo_Focus_Base_Weapon`, `_Element`, `_Defense`, all under `Mmo_Focus_Base`, none in a set), which carries the kind's line and guard stats, its descriptions and the step amounts (`Vow`, `PathLine`, `PathGuard`, `HonedOne`, `HonedTwo`: 5 each, the Bulwark 10 and its guard face 0). A pack adds a Focus with one row file. Since a row binds only plain values, the template writes its three grant list shapes (a line bound `While` `MmoWeaponSkill`, an unbound line or one resistance, a weapon's two resistances) and the Signature's `Requires` under keys the rows fill: one becomes `Grants` (or `Requires`), the rest `$Comment`, which the codec skips.
- **Prices are four tier skeletons.** `TrackPicks/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/Mmo_Mastery_Tier_I` to `IV` (1, 2, 3, 4 Mastery Points and 250, 1000, 2250, 4000 Essence of Life, `Cost.Price` only, no `Cost.Points`) under `Mmo_Mastery` (Abstract: `ResetsAs` Mastery, `Where` the trainer's two placements). Vow is tier I, both Path faces II, Signature and Honed I III, Honed II IV: a Focus is 13 Mastery Points and 9750 Essence of Life. The steps are decoded over the skeletons as the pack ships them, so an owner entry on a skeleton switches its steps off (`Enabled` false, through the lineage) but does not reprice them; an owner retunes one step by its own id.
- **Lines.** A weapon's line is its own damage type (`DamageBonus_Physical`; Archery and Artillery `DamageBonus_Projectile`), bound `While` `MmoWeaponSkill` on its home skill, never the every-type `DamageBonus`; its guard face is `DamageResist_Physical` and `DamageResist_Projectile`, 5 each. An element's line is `DamageBonus_<Element>`, its guard `DamageResist_<Element>`. The Bulwark's line is Health, 10 a step; its guard face grants nothing and opens the Guardian's Call ability mod. The Signature grants nothing. Every step but the Vow names the Vow as its `Stance.Face`, so it counts only while that Focus is sworn.
- **Order.** Each Path face needs the Vow, Honed I the Signature and Honed II Honed I (`Prerequisites`); the Signature needs either Path face through its one `Requires`, `ziggfreedcommon:track_picks_taken` on `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Path_*` Min 1, an ownership reading, so an admin's teach or the converter teaching in order passes it with no oath sworn. Owning `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Honed_II` is a finished Focus: the Second Oath's `Mmo_Focus_*_Honed_II` reads exactly those nineteen ids.
- **The oath set**, `TrackStances/MMOSkillTree/Mmo_Focus.json`: slot `I` open, slot `II` on `Mmo_Focus_Slot_II`; kinds `Weapon` (Max 1, Health -25), `Element` (Max 2, Mana -50) and `Defense` (Max 1, `DamageDealt` -10, a true tenth of every hit dealt); `Break` 500 `Life_Essence`, `Hold` free; `Where` the two trainer placements; `Budget` `Combat` 25 and `Health` 40 (a weapon's guard face, 10 Combat points, is the face the either-or weighs).
- **The second oath's key**, `TrackPicks/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/Mmo_Focus_Slot_II.json` on Defense, `TaughtOnly`, under `Mmo_Mastery`.
- **Text:** `mastery.focus.<focus>.name`, `mastery.focus.step.<step>` (a step's name, `{0}` the Focus; the Signature reads its own `mastery.focus.<focus>.signature`), one description a step and kind (`mastery.focus.step.<vow|path_line|path_guard|honed>.<weapon|element|defense>.desc`), `mastery.focus.step.signature.desc` and `.honed_ii.desc`, `mastery.oath.*` (the oath page's name, line and kind names) and `mastery.focus.slot_ii.*`, none with a number in it.

### Focus signatures

- **Every Focus has a signature**, opened by its Signature step (`<Home track>/Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Signature`) and live only while that Focus is sworn. Fourteen are CommonLib procs under `Procs/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/`: one abstract skeleton, `Mmo_Focus_Signature` (`Trigger` Hit, `Hit.OverTime` false, `Order` 10 so a signature runs after every talent's proc on the same hit), and one child a Focus, `Mmo_Focus_<Focus>_Signature` with `Parent` `Mmo_Focus_Signature`, holding only its `Requires` (`ziggfreedcommon:track_pick` on the Signature step, which reads only a sworn Focus since stances), its `Like` (the Focus's own damage line), a weapon's `Holder` (`MmoWeaponSkill`), its wait if it has one and its actions. A pack adds a signature for its own Focus with one more child file.
- **The procs:** Artillery's Demolition Expert (an Artillery hit where the Projectile line counts flinches the struck foe and every foe within 3 blocks of it, every 6 s), Blunt's Skullbreaker (a charged mace swing staggers for 1 s, `Zig_Staggered`), Polearms' Spearpoint (a hit at 2.5 blocks or more roots for 1 s, `Root`), Staves' Quarterstaff (a staff swing pushes every foe within 2.5 blocks back about 2 blocks, every 10 s), Swords' Masterwork Blade (a sword `Crit` breaks the foe's guard and makes it flinch, 6 s a foe), Unarmed's Iron Body (a fist combo's `Finisher` gives back 2 stamina, every 6 s), Ice's Deep Freeze (15% on an Ice hit to freeze for 1 s, `Ability_Frost_Freeze`), Lightning's Thunderhead (staggers the struck foe and every foe within 2.5 blocks for 0.5 s, every 10 s), Water's High Tide (pushes back about 2 blocks and slows for 1.5 s, `Ability_Frost_Slow`, 10 s a foe), Arcane's Leyline (slows to half speed for 2 s, `Slow`), Void's Event Horizon (pulls the struck foe and every foe within 4 blocks about a block toward you, every 10 s), Poison's Blightbloom (a killing Poison hit spreads `Poison_T1` at half damage to up to 3 foes within 3 blocks, every 8 s), Earth's Tectonic (lifts the foe about a block and a half, 10 s a foe) and Life's Transfusion (withers the foe for 2 s and heals the nearest hurt ally within 12 blocks for 5, never you, every 10 s). Every control lands through CommonLib's control landing by its effect's kind, a talent's, so a boss or an elite shrugs it; the poison and the heal are no control. Skullbreaker, Spearpoint, Deep Freeze and Leyline keep no wait of their own: their own trigger paces them and the foe's Immunity, which each landing ramps, stops a lock (held shorter each time, then shrugged for a while). High Tide keeps its 10 s a foe, which paces its push; its slow shares it.
- **Five are ability mods** under `MMOSkillTree/AbilityMods/Mastery/`, one file each on the same shape (`TargetAbility`, `Talent.Pick` on the Signature step, `PerRank` false): Archery's Hawkshot (Piercing Shot +1 `pierceCount`, +5% `damageMultiplier`), Axes' Tempest (Tempest -2000 `cooldownMs`), Daggers' Voidwalk (Shadowstep -3000 `cooldownMs`), Fire's Pyroclasm (Fireball +1 `damageRadius`, -400 `cooldownMs`) and the Bulwark's Mountainshield (Shield Slam +500 stun `durationMs`, +200 `iframesDurationMs`). A sixth, `Mmo_Focus_Bulwark_Path_Guard`, is the Bulwark's Path guard face: Guardian's Call +40% `durationMs`. An ability mod reads its pick through MMO Skill Tree's talent read, which counts an owned pick until that read moves onto CommonLib's live count.
- **Names and lines:** `mastery.focus.<focus>.signature` and `.desc` in the pack's `mmoskilltree.lang`, one pair a Focus, for the Signature step's text; the lines carry no numbers. Every number is retuned, or a signature switched off, in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/procs.json` (procs) or by a same-id ability mod file.
