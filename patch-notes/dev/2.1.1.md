- **The Mastery Point milestones become a per-skill achievement ladder.** `CommandRewards/MMOSkillMasteryPack.json` and `CommandRewardTemplates/Mastery_Point_Milestones.json` are gone, and with them the Control file's `CommandRewards` and `CommandRewardTemplates` keys. In their place, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Achievements/MMOSkillTree/Mastery/PerSkill/` holds `Mastery_Point_Base` (Abstract: Category `mastery`, Points 10, `Requires` the `mmoskilltree:feature` `mastery` factor, criterion `reach-level-skill` as `REACH_LEVEL` on `{skill}` EXACT, `Rewards.Claim` one `mastery_point`) and the thirteen rungs `Mastery_Point_15` to `Mastery_Point_195`. Each rung takes the base through `Parent` and sets `Abstract`, its `Amount`, a `SortOrder` from 100 to 112, its tier on the chain `{skill}_mastery_points` and `Meta.mmoskilltree.PerSkill.IdPattern` `{skill}_mastery_<level>`, so the plugin stamps it out for every listable skill, custom skills included.
- **Existing players are offered every rung they have passed.** `REACH_LEVEL` is the plugin's alias onto `STAT_THRESHOLD`, which the achievement engine re-reads at self-heal (login, and opening an achievement surface), so each rung at or below a player's level is earned there and waits to be collected. Nothing reads the old command-reward claim record, so a point collected from a skill's rewards page before is paid again.
- **New keys:** `achievement.mastery_points.title` (`{0} Level {1}`) and `achievement.mastery_points.desc` (`Reach {0} level {1}.`), `{0}` the skill's name and `{1}` the rung's level. English only so far.
- Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1, whose per-skill sort stride of 16 keeps one skill's thirteen rungs together on the page.
