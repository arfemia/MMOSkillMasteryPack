A picture for every damage school and every quest, the trainer keeping her own quests, and one fix for the Mastery Trainer. Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1.

- **Each damage school track carries its own picture.** Fire, Ice, Lightning, Void and Water are drawn with the matching essence; Arcane takes the mastery crystal and Poison a venom sac, neither school having an essence of its own in the game. All seven drew the same iron bar before, because a track that targets every ability has nothing to take a picture from. The six ability tracks drop an `Icon` line that named a UI texture rather than an item and was never drawn; each still shows its ability's own picture. Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1, which draws a track's authored icon.

- **Every quest has a picture.** All seven name one, drawn beside the quest wherever it is listed and on the notice when it finishes: the mastery crystal for meeting the trainer and for the tithe, life essence for the three essence quests, a book pile for the two level climbs.
- **Fixed: A Word with the Trainer is collected at the trainer, and listed on nobody else's screen.** Once finished it sat under "Ready to collect" at every character on the server, Sawyer Marn included. It names the trainer.
- **First Step Toward Mastery and Path of Mastery keep their report-back step off the list until the climbing is done**, so "Return to the trainer" is not sitting under the levels as though both were open.
- **The Mastery Tithe is written the same way as the other six quests**, on the shared quest schema. It asks for everything it asked for before: a six-hour wait between tithes, and the mastery feature, 500 total levels and the hoarder achievement to open it. Nobody's progress moves.
- **The repeating quests stop labelling themselves.** Every quest list badges a quest Daily, Weekly or Repeatable off its own timing, so the hand-written tags are gone; the tithe's Daily tag went with them, since six hours is not a day and the badge reads the timing itself.
- **Fixed: the Mastery Trainer shrugs off environmental damage, as she was always meant to.** Her role asked for the Immunity_Environmental effect under a key the engine does not read (`EntityEffect`), so the request was silently ignored and the server logged an unused-key warning at boot. The role authors `EntityEffects` as the list the engine expects, the same spelling the base game's own passive NPCs use.
