One fix for the Mastery Trainer.

- **Fixed: the Mastery Trainer shrugs off environmental damage, as she was always meant to.** Her role asked for the Immunity_Environmental effect under a key the engine does not read (`EntityEffect`), so the request was silently ignored and the server logged an unused-key warning at boot. The role now authors `EntityEffects` as the list the engine expects, the same spelling the base game's own passive NPCs use.
