# Changelog - MMO Skill Mastery Pack

## [2.0.0] - UNRELEASED (HELD)

- **Every mastery file rewritten onto the plugin's structured mastery schema.** Two `Abstract` bases (`Mastery_Base`, `Mastery_School_Base`) reused through native `Parent`, fifteen hand-authored tracks, and the three per-skill families (combat, gathering, utility) each written once as a `MasteryGenerators/` file the plugin stamps out per skill. The old template files are gone.
- **Track content is unchanged in play.** Every node id, price, gate and bonus carries across, so purchased nodes, respec state and stat sacrifices are untouched.
- **Modifier entries author the canonical PascalCase grammar**: `ParamKey` (the older `Key` spelling still parses), the `SET` shape where a value is replaced outright, and a `Condition` carried directly on the modifier it gates (nested `SelfHp`/`TargetHp` groups) instead of a wrapper entry.
- **The Fireball pierce node (Burning Pierce) grants +1 pierce on top of Fireball's own base**, stacking with any other pierce bonus, instead of overwriting the ability's pierce total.
- **The pack's quests and achievements move onto the shared progression shape**: an achievement's `Criteria` is a map keyed by criterion id, and every payout goes through the one `Rewards` group (`Auto` pays on the spot, `Claim` waits to be collected). Behavior-preserving; nobody's progress is re-pointed.
- **This pack owns the Mastery Trainer end to end.** Their model, their role body, their nameplate and press-F hint keys (`Server/Languages/<bcp47>/npcs.lang`), their Forgotten Temple placement and their conversation all ship here now; the mod jar carries none of them, so a server without this pack stands no trainer up and reaches the Mastery screen from the hub menu instead. Nothing changes in play for a server that has the pack: its placement and conversation already won over the jar's, and they simply stop being overrides.
- **Adds A Word with the Trainer**, a short introduction quest that starts the moment the Adventurer's Guide has been met in the temple and asks the player to cross the landing and speak to the trainer standing there. The trainer answers with a line written for a first meeting, then drops into their usual menu; it pays one mastery point, enough to spend on the screen it just introduced. It needs the mastery feature switched on and the jar's temple meeting finished, so it never appears on a server where the trainer is not standing.
- **Adds The Trainer's Due**, a calendar daily at the Mastery Trainer that refreshes at the day boundary rather than on a rolling timer. Pay four life essence and three linen scrap, and take one roll of a four-roll table: essence always, a mastery point about two days in five, a short all-skill experience boost about one day in ten, and about one day in thirty-three the **Adept's Staff**, a copy of the trainer's own bo that cannot be crafted and carries a little Health and Stamina while held. The mastery-point roll is the number to watch when retuning; everything else is pocket change beside it.
- **The Trainer's Due celebrates its rare pull.** The staff roll authors the `Rare_Find` cue, so the moment the Adept's Staff actually lands the player gets the gold toast and legendary-chest chime the MMO jar ships under that name; a cue never fires over an empty hand. Author the same `Cue` on a rare roll of your own to reuse the celebration, or a new cue id plus a `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/FeedbackMoments` file of that name for one of your own.
- Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.0+ and ZiggfreedCommon 2.0.0+. A pre-2.0.0 copy of this pack has no readable tracks on those plugin versions; update the pack and the plugin together.
