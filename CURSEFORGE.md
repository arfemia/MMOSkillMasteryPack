# MMO Skill Mastery Pack

Adds mastery to [MMO Skill Tree](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree): 28 tracks that keep making your skills stronger once their skill tree is done, and a Mastery Trainer with daily lessons.

A free content pack. It needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 or newer and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, on a Hytale Update 7 server.

## What it adds

- 28 mastery tracks: one for each combat skill, Mining, Woodcutting, Harvesting and Enchanting, six for signature abilities (Fireball, Meteor, Whirlwind, Piercing Shot, Shield Slam and Shadowstep), and seven for damage schools (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Water, Arcane, Void and Poison).
- Each track has a few big nodes that change how its skill plays, then an Eternal node you can buy again and again.
- A school track counts that damage type whatever deals it. Buy Fire and it pays on a fireball and on a molten hammer swing alike. Each school also sells resistance to itself.
- Mastery Points. Every skill pays one at level 15, 30, 45 and on up to 195, waiting in the Mastery section of your achievement book.
- Essence of Life, the game's own item, which you spend on nodes beside your Mastery Points. You can trade it, and you drop it when you die.
- The Mastery Trainer in the Forgotten Temple, with new lessons every day and a standing that climbs from Newcomer to Grandmaster.
- Eight quests and six achievements, most of them paying Mastery Points.
- With the [MMO Skill Bounty Pack](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree-bounty-board-pack-rich-customizable), the Token Shop sells you up to three Mastery Points a day for Bounty Tokens.
- Translated into 9 languages.

## How it works

1. Reach the Forgotten Temple and talk to the Adventurer's Guide. He sends you across the landing to the Mastery Trainer.
2. Press F on the trainer and choose "Show me my mastery." to see your tracks. Buy a node with Mastery Points and Essence of Life.
3. Ask "Do you have lessons for me?" for the day's lessons: fell logs, mine ore, catch fish, hunt, use your abilities or craft at a bench. Bring each one back to his board for Essence of Life and standing.
4. Each rank of standing pays Mastery Points: 3 at Student, rising to 25 at Grandmaster, which also gives you the Grandmaster title. Ask "How am I doing?" to see your rank.

## For server owners

- Without this pack, MMO Skill Tree runs as normal and hides its Mastery tab.
- The Fishing and Acrobatics tracks ship switched off. Turn one on in `mods/mmoskilltree/mastery.json`, where you can also retune any node.
- Every track, quest, rank and lesson is a file in the pack. Ship a file with the same name in your own pack to change one. The [mastery docs](https://mmo-skill-tree-docs.ziggfreed.com/docs/mastery/) cover the rest.
